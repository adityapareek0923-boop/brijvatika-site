Refund policy

Last updated: October 2026

Our refund policy is straightforward and designed to be fair in both directions.

Reschedules

Free reschedule up to 24 hours before your scheduled session. Use the link in your confirmation email, or reply directly to it.

Cancellations

When you cancelRefund
More than 24 hours before the session100%
Within 24 hours of the session50%
No-show (missed the session without notice)None
We cannot find a mutually workable time within 14 days of your booking100%
Technical failure on our side (we couldn't deliver the session)100%

Annual Guidance program

Full refund within 7 days of purchase if no sessions have been held. After the first session, remaining sessions are refundable pro-rata minus a 10% administration fee.

How to request

Email hello@brijvatika.com with your booking confirmation number. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Disputed charges

If you believe a charge is incorrect, email us first — we'll resolve it quickly. We handle Stripe disputes directly and will respond with full documentation.
