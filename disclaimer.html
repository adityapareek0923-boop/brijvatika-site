What Vedic astrology is — and is not

Jyotish (Vedic astrology) is a traditional system of interpreting planetary patterns based on your birth chart. Acharya Brijesh Pareek offers consultations in that tradition for personal insight and reflection on the themes shaping your life.

A Vedic astrology consultation is not:

  • Medical, psychiatric, or psychological diagnosis or treatment
  • Financial, investment, tax, or legal advice
  • A guarantee of specific future outcomes
  • A substitute for the counsel of a qualified professional in any of the above fields

Decisions remain yours

Any decision you make — about health, money, relationships, career, legal matters, or anything else — is your own responsibility. Please consult qualified professionals for professional advice. The Acharya's guidance is offered as one perspective among many you may consider.

Mental health and crisis

If you are experiencing a mental-health crisis or thoughts of self-harm, please contact a mental-health professional, a crisis helpline, or emergency services in your country immediately. Brij Vatika is not an appropriate first resource for crisis support.

Children

Consultations are offered for adults (18+). Chart readings for children are provided on request only with the explicit consent of a parent or guardian who is also present during the consultation.

Religious and cultural respect

Brij Vatika is a Jyotish practice rooted in Vedic tradition. We welcome clients of all backgrounds and beliefs. Nothing in our consultations is intended to require or displace any religious practice of your own.
