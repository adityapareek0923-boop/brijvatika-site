About

Acharya Brijesh Pareek

[Acharya portrait]

A life rooted in Jyotish.

Jaipur · Serving clients worldwide

For four decades, Acharya Brijesh Pareek has read the stars with the same discipline he brought to building cities. A Jyotishi in the classical tradition and a long-serving officer with the Government of Rajasthan, his contributions to the state's urban development, water conservation, and heritage programmes are recorded across a shelf of published volumes — from the Jaipur Municipal Corporation to the Rajasthan Urban Infrastructure Development Project. Few astrologers come to the chart with that kind of record behind them.

Based in Jaipur — the city Sawai Jai Singh laid out by Vedic principle three centuries ago — the Acharya reads a horoscope the way the śāstras intend: not as a verdict, but as a map of tendencies, timing, and remedies. He counsels seekers from India and abroad on marriage and family, career direction, the timing of major decisions, and year-ahead guidance. Each reading is grounded in the Parāśari system, cross-checked against transit and daśā, and delivered with the patience of a scholar and the clarity of a man who has spent a lifetime translating complexity into counsel.

FAQ

Questions
asked first.

How do I book a consultation?
Choose a tier, pay via Stripe in your local currency, then fill the short birth-details form on the confirmation page. We confirm your session slot by email within 24 hours.
What information do you need for a reading?
Full name, date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth (city, state/country). Time of birth accuracy to within 10 minutes is strongly recommended. If you don't know the time, we can discuss alternate approaches.
What language are consultations in?
Hindi or English — your choice. The Acharya is fluent in both and will adapt to whichever is comfortable for you.
What if I need to reschedule?
Reschedule up to 24 hours before your session at no cost through the email we send you. Cancellations within 24 hours are 50% refundable. No-shows are non-refundable.
Do you offer remedies and rituals?
Yes, where appropriate. The Acharya may suggest mantras, charitable acts, or specific rituals based on your chart. Any suggested puja or ceremony is optional and separately priced.
Is this religious guidance or counseling?
Vedic astrology (Jyotish) is a traditional system of pattern-reading and timing. Consultations are offered for personal insight and reflection. They are not a substitute for medical, financial, or legal advice — those are decisions for qualified professionals.
How do I pay?
Secure one-time Stripe payment in your local currency (USD, CAD, INR). We never store card details. Payment confirmation and birth-details form arrive by email within minutes.
