Questions

Asked first,
answered honestly.

How does the booking work?
Choose a consultation area, pick a date and time from the Acharya's live calendar, share your birth details or Vastu questions, and submit. For now, payment is handled offline — you'll receive confirmation and a payment link within 24 hours. Online payment via UPI, cards, and international checkout is coming soon.
What birth details do I need?
Full birth name, date of birth, exact time of birth (to within 10 minutes if possible), and city of birth. If you don't know your exact birth time, we can discuss alternate approaches such as birth-time rectification.
What about Vastu — do I need a floor plan?
For a basic Vastu consultation, no. For a detailed reading of your home or office, uploading a floor plan (even a hand-drawn one with directions marked) gives the Acharya far more to work with.
Can I bring a family member's chart?
Yes. During booking, let us know whether the consultation is for yourself, a child, a spouse, or another family member. For married couples' matching (guna milan), share both charts.
What language will the consultation be in?
Hindi or English — your choice. The Acharya is fluent in both and will match whichever is comfortable for you.
What if I need to reschedule?
Reschedule up to 24 hours before your session at no cost through the email we send you. Within 24 hours, we accommodate where possible.
Does Vedic astrology replace medical, legal, or financial advice?
No. Vedic astrology is offered for personal insight and reflection. For medical, legal, or financial decisions, please consult qualified professionals in those fields.
