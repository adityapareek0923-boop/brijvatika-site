Why data matters

Nothing read from
thin air. Everything
from your chart.

Vedic consultation is not a performance. It is a reading — of planetary positions calculated from the exact moment of your birth, and of the Vastu lines of your home or office.

The Acharya does not improvise. He computes, reads, cross-checks against transit and dasha, and tells you what the data shows. If a question cannot be answered from the chart, he says so.

  • Full birth name, date, time, and place — the foundation of every reading
  • Chart computed using the Parāśari system and Lahiri ayanamsa
  • Dasha, antardasha, and transit layered on the natal chart
  • For Vastu — floor plan, orientation, and direction-by-direction analysis
  • Remedies given are specific to your chart, not generic prescriptions

How does the booking work?
Choose a consultation area, pick a date and time from the Acharya's live calendar, share your birth details or Vastu questions, and submit. For now, payment is handled offline — you'll receive confirmation and a payment link within 24 hours. Online payment via UPI, cards, and international checkout is being added shortly.
What birth details do I need?
Full birth name, date of birth, exact time of birth (within 10 minutes if possible), and city of birth. These are the inputs the Acharya uses to compute your chart. If you don't know your exact birth time, we can discuss birth-time rectification during the session.
What about Vastu — do I need a floor plan?
For a basic Vastu consultation, no. For a detailed reading of your home or office, a floor plan (even hand-drawn, with directions marked) gives the Acharya far more to work with.
What language is the consultation in?
All consultations are conducted in Hindi. The Acharya reads Sanskrit śāstras and speaks fluent Hindi; he does not conduct sessions in English. The website is in English for clarity, but the session itself will be in Hindi.
Can I bring a family member's chart?
Yes. During booking, let us know whether the consultation is for yourself, a child, a spouse, or another family member. For married couples' matching (guna milan), share both charts.
What if I need to reschedule?
Reschedule up to 24 hours before your session at no cost through the email we send you. Within 24 hours, we accommodate where possible.
Does Vedic consultation replace medical, legal, or financial advice?
No. Vedic consultation is offered for personal insight and reflection. For medical, legal, or financial decisions, please consult qualified professionals in those fields.
